## Blade Pass Frequency (BPF) Physics

The fundamental acoustic frequency generated by a propeller blade rotating through the air column is determined by blade count and shaft velocity:

$$f_{\text{BPF}} = \frac{N_{\text{blades}} \cdot \text{RPM}}{60}$$

Aerodynamic turbulence and blade vortex interactions generate integer harmonics at frequencies:

$$f_k = k \cdot f_{\text{BPF}} = k \cdot \frac{N_{\text{blades}} \cdot \text{RPM}}{60}, \quad k \in \{1, 2, \dots, K\}$$

For a standard 4-blade quadrotor turning at 5,420 RPM:
- Fundamental $f_1 = (4 \cdot 5420) / 60 = 361.3\text{ Hz}$
- 2nd Harmonic $f_2 = 722.7\text{ Hz}$
- 3rd Harmonic $f_3 = 1084.0\text{ Hz}$
- 4th Harmonic $f_4 = 1445.3\text{ Hz}$

These harmonics directly obliterate the primary formant regions of human speech ($F_1 \approx 300\text{--}900\text{ Hz}$ and $F_2 \approx 800\text{--}2500\text{ Hz}$).

## Digital Filter Topology

Sonon constructs a cascaded bank of Transposed Direct Form II IIR biquad notch filters:

$$H(z) = \prod_{k=1}^K \frac{b_{0,k} + b_{1,k} z^{-1} + b_{2,k} z^{-2}}{1 + a_{1,k} z^{-1} + a_{2,k} z^{-2}}$$

Where the digital coefficients are parameterized by center frequency $\omega_k$ and quality factor $Q$:

$$\omega_k = \frac{2\pi f_k}{f_s}, \quad \alpha_k = \frac{\sin(\omega_k)}{2 Q}$$

$$b_{0,k} = 1, \quad b_{1,k} = -2\cos(\omega_k), \quad b_{2,k} = 1$$

$$a_{0,k} = 1 + \alpha_k, \quad a_{1,k} = -2\cos(\omega_k), \quad a_{2,k} = 1 - \alpha_k$$

Normalizing by $a_{0,k}$ produces the transposed state-space update:

$$y[n] = b_0 x[n] + s_1[n-1]$$

$$s_1[n] = b_1 x[n] - a_1 y[n] + s_2[n-1]$$

$$s_2[n] = b_2 x[n] - a_2 y[n]$$

## Filter Parameter Specifications

| Parameter | Type | Default | Unit | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `blade_count` | `u32` | `4` | Blades | Physical propeller blade count per motor |
| `max_harmonics` | `usize` | `8` | Count | Maximum harmonic notch filters instantiated |
| `q_factor` | `f32` | `48.0` | Dimensionless | Notch filter quality factor ($f_0 / \Delta f$) |
| `min_rpm` | `f32` | `1000.0` | RPM | Lower tracking threshold for ESC telemetry |
| `max_rpm` | `f32` | `24000.0` | RPM | Upper tracking threshold (micro-drones / racers) |
| `recompute_us` | `f32` | `4.1` | us | Measured coefficient recalculation time |

::: info ARCHITECTURE DIRECTIVE: TELEMETRY SYNC
When MAVLink telemetry drops or latency exceeds 50 ms, Sonon falls back to a spectral autocorrelation estimator to track rotor harmonic fundamental frequencies from the microphone audio stream directly.
:::
